## 7. New Pricing page: /pricing
- Add the route in App.tsx, and a "Pricing" link in the header nav (components/layout.tsx, beside About) and the footer.
- Headline "Simple pricing. Free for filmmakers." Subline "Providers pay to be listed. Give indie filmmakers a promo code and pay less."
- Plan cards from /listing-config, in this order: Indie Partner ("Most popular"), Standard, Lifetime Indie Partner (shows "X of 10 spots left", or sold out), Nonprofit (show both nonprofit prices on one card), Government.
- Each card: price, who it's for, what's included, and a "Get listed" button to /get-listed?plan=<plan>.
- Show "Lock in your price for life" only when specialActive is true, as the form already does.
- FAQ below the cards:
  1. What counts as an indie promo code? Any discount or deal for independent productions: a lower day rate, a weekday discount, a free add-on, or a budget-based package. You choose the code and the deal.
  2. Why use a promo code? When a filmmaker uses your code, you know the booking came from the Indie Film Guide.
  3. What does "locked for life" mean? If you sign up during the launch special, your yearly price never goes up as long as your listing renews without a break. If it's canceled, or a renewal fails and isn't fixed within 30 days, the lock ends.
  4. What does Lifetime include? One payment, no renewals. Your listing stays up for as long as the Indie Film Guide operates, as long as it stays accurate and you keep honoring your promo code. It can't be transferred to another business. If the guide shuts down within 3 years of your purchase, you get a prorated refund.
  5. Do nonprofits get a discount? Yes. Verified nonprofits pay $99/yr with a promo code or $199/yr without one. We check your EIN before approving.
  6. Does paying change where I show up? No. Results are always alphabetical.
  7. Can I change my code? Yes, anytime. Removing your code moves you to the Standard price at renewal.
