---
name: Cancellation and refund policies
description: Owner-confirmed timing for annual cancellations and review of refund requests.
---
Annual cancellations take effect at the end of the paid period.

**Why:** The owner selected “End of its paid period.”

**How to apply:** Preserve the remaining paid listing period when stopping annual renewals; do not treat a request to cancel as authorization for immediate removal.

Refund requests are reviewed case by case.

**Why:** The owner selected “Case by case.”

**How to apply:** Do not invent a fixed refund window, blanket no-refund policy, guaranteed refund, or automatic refund approval. Refunding a payment and stopping future renewals remain separate actions.
