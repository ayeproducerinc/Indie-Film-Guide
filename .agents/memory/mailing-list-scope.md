name: Mailing list scope and consent
description: Owner-selected capture-only first version and consent boundaries.
---
The owner selected “Save privately in admin with an export option” instead of connecting an email marketing service for the first version.

**Why:** The immediate goal is capturing filmmaker and creative artist contacts, not adding an email-sending system.

**How to apply:** Keep mailing-list contacts separate from business-listing contact information; listing submissions do not imply marketing consent. Treat public signups as self-submitted, not verified email ownership. Do not add sending, verification emails, or an external mailing service without a new request.

Anonymous repeat signups must not reactivate an existing opted-out contact or overwrite its consent evidence.

**Why:** Anyone can enter someone else's email into an unverified public form; silently restoring withdrawn consent would allow abuse.

**How to apply:** Preserve withdrawal status during duplicate requests. A future re-subscription flow needs an explicit, trustworthy consent process rather than simply removing this protection.
