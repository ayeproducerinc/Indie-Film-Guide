Website and Phone must be separate required fields, not optional.

**Why:** The user explicitly repeated that website and phone are required, superseding the initial brief.

**How to apply:** Enforce both in public submissions and server validation; do not label either optional.

Phone numbers must be formatted as `(xxx) xxx-xxxx` everywhere they are shown, including public directory listings and all admin views/forms.

**Why:** The user explicitly corrected partial coverage and required formatting in ALL places where the phone number exists, including the directory listing.

**How to apply:** Audit all phone displays, labels and form defaults whenever working with contact details. Preserve canonical stored numbers and functional tap-to-call targets; format the user-facing text.

Email may be shown publicly only when the provider checks “List email publicly.” Update wording elsewhere that says emails are never public.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested an opt-in checkbox instead of unconditional email secrecy.

**How to apply:** Default to private for new and existing listings. Preserve the consent setting through save/admin editing, and exclude email from public data when consent is absent.

EIN verification must be a separate button labeled "Verify EIN" in the nonprofit listing's Private details, alongside the saved EIN. Do not require opening Edit or saving/reopening a form to verify it.

**Why:** The user explicitly rejected the edit-checkbox verification flow.

**How to apply:** Keep EIN editing separate from admin verification of the saved number. Show private EIN/status and an explicit verification action; never auto-verify public input.

Listing changes are requested by support email and applied by the administrator; do not add a business change-request form or self-service editing for this scope.

**Why:** The owner rejected a request form because they would not know whether the requester represents the business, and chose email instructions requesting name, business phone number and the requested changes.

**How to apply:** Keep professional email-based update instructions visible. Do not treat receipt of an email as proof of authorization or automatically publish requested changes.
