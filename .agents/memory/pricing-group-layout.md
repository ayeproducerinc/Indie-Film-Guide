---
name: Pricing group layout
description: Owner-approved grouping for the three organization pricing tabs on desktop and mobile.
---

Use compact tabs labeled For-Profit, Non-Profit and Government above a single bordered pricing-group panel with a matching heading. Business cards sit side by side on desktop and stack within the panel on mobile; the three tabs stay together above it.

**Why:** The owner found equal-width desktop tabs confusing because they lined up with the three business price cards, appearing to label individual cards. They approved the grouped desktop and mobile ASCII layouts and said to "lock it in."

**How to apply:** Keep organization selection visually separate from plan selection. Do not stretch desktop organization tabs into three columns aligned with the three business cards. Preserve complete pricing content and existing signup behavior.
