---
name: Temporary payment waiver scope
description: Why a listing can be live without a Stripe payment and why the exception is narrow.
---

The owner requested a temporary payment bypass for the single existing listing they had tried to approve, not a global bypass of paid plans.

**Why:** Stripe setup was deferred, but the owner wanted that listing published now. A waiver must not misrepresent collected revenue or authorize later automatic charging.

**How to apply:** Preserve the explicit Payment waived state until the owner asks to reverse it. Other paid-plan submissions still require confirmed payment; do not extend the exception to future listings or interpret it as permission to remove Stripe.
