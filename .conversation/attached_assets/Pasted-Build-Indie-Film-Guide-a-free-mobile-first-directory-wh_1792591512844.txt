Build "Indie Film Guide": a free, mobile-first directory where independent filmmakers find local resources in Georgia, and providers pay a yearly fee to be listed. Keep it simple and fast. Use Replit's built-in PostgreSQL database and Stripe for payments.

## Who uses it
- Filmmakers: browse for free, no account or sign-up.
- Providers (businesses, nonprofits, government offices): fill out a short form to get listed.
- Admin (me): approves or rejects listings before they go live.

## Categories (exactly these 6)
1. Locations
2. Equipment & Props
3. Talent, Crew & On-Set
4. Business Services & Licensing (short label on filter buttons: "Business & Licensing")
5. Funding & Banking
6. Distribution & Marketing

## Stages (exactly these 4; a listing can have several)
Idea, Production, Post-Production, Distribution

## Listing fields
- Business name (required)
- What you offer (required, max 200 characters)
- Category (required, pick one)
- Stage(s) (required, pick at least one)
- City (required). Special values: "Statewide" (Georgia-wide, e.g. state film office) and "Nationwide" (national companies).
- Website (optional), phone (optional), email (required, never shown publicly)
- Provider type: Business, Nonprofit, or Government
- Indie rate offered: Yes / No
- Indie rate details (required if Yes, one line)
- Plan: Indie Partner, Standard, or Government
- Status: Pending, Live, Rejected
- Payment status: Unpaid, Paid, Not required
- Founding price locked (true/false), created date, approved date

## Pricing
- Indie Partner: $199/year (provider offers an indie filmmaker rate)
- Standard: $499/year (no indie rate)
- Government offices: free
- Nonprofits pay the same as businesses.
- Launch special: anyone who signs up before SPECIAL_END_DATE keeps their price for life. Store SPECIAL_END_DATE as an environment variable and show "Lock in your price for life" on the form only before that date.

## Pages

### 1. Home / directory (public)
- Header: "Indie Film Guide" logo text, and a "Get Listed" button.
- Headline: "Every indie film resource in Georgia, in one place." Subline: "Locations, gear, crew, legal, funding and distribution, sorted by where your project is. Free for filmmakers."
- Search box (searches name and description).
- Filters: City dropdown (built from cities of Live listings, excluding Statewide/Nationwide), Category dropdown, Stage dropdown, "Indie rate only" checkbox.
- Category chips across the top (All + the 6 categories) that act as a quick filter.
- Results as cards: badges, name, category · city, stage tags, short description, "View details" button.
- When a city is selected, also show Statewide and Nationwide listings.
- Show a result count. If no results: "No resources match these filters yet."
- Only Live listings appear publicly.

### 2. Listing detail (public)
- Name, badges, category, city, stages, description.
- "Indie filmmaker rate" section with their details, or "This provider hasn't posted an indie rate."
- Contact buttons: Call (tel:), Website, and "Email" via a contact form that sends to the provider without exposing their address (if email sending is too much for v1, hide the Email button).
- Give each listing a clean URL like /listing/rjr-props.

### 3. Get Listed form (public)
- The fields above, in this order: name, what you offer, category, stages, city, website, phone, email, provider type, indie rate question.
- Indie rate question with three options shown as selectable cards:
  - "Yes, Indie Partner, $199/yr" (reveals "Describe your indie rate" field)
  - "No, Standard, $499/yr"
  - "Government office, Free"
- Clear inline validation messages.
- On submit: save as Pending. Government goes to a "Submitted for review" page. Others go to Stripe Checkout.

### 4. Payment
- Use Stripe Checkout with a yearly subscription (two Prices: $199/yr and $499/yr; store the Price IDs as env vars).
- Use a Stripe webhook to mark the listing Paid when checkout completes. Do not rely on the success page alone.
- Success page: "You're in. Your listing goes live within 24 hours. Your price is locked for life." (drop the "locked for life" line after SPECIAL_END_DATE)
- Cancel page: "Your listing is saved but not paid yet," with a button to try again.

### 5. Admin (password-protected with ADMIN_PASSWORD env var)
- Tabs: Pending, Live, Rejected.
- Each row: name, category, city, plan, payment status badge (Paid green, Unpaid red, Free blue), submitted date.
- Actions: Approve (sets Live), Reject, Edit any field, Delete.
- Block approving an Unpaid paid-plan listing, with a message: "Not paid yet. Approve after payment comes in."
- Admin can also add a listing directly (for government offices I add myself).
- Show simple totals at the top: Live listings, Paid listings, yearly revenue.

### 6. Simple pages
- About: one paragraph on what the guide is.
- Terms: listings are paid advertising, a listing doesn't guarantee bookings, payment doesn't affect where a listing appears, and providers are responsible for honoring their posted indie rate.
- Footer links to About, Terms, and Get Listed.

## Rules
- Paying never changes search order. Sort results alphabetically, with Indie Partner and Government listings shown the same as Standard. (No featured or sponsored placement in v1.)
- Never show a provider's email publicly.
- Seed the database with no fake businesses. Leave an empty state per category: "No [category] listed yet. Are you a provider? Get Listed."

## Design
- Mobile-first, clean, fast. Works perfectly at 375px wide with no sideways scrolling.
- Badges: "Indie rate" in green, "Government" in blue.
- Use a bold condensed display font for headings and a clean sans-serif for body text. Neutral background with one strong blue accent.
- Light and dark mode following the device setting.
